All teachers — Allow all-date entry

Off by default: once a day ends, only Admins can add or edit that day's attendance. Turn this on to let every House Faculty Coordinator mark attendance for any fest day at once, not just today. Off by default: attendance can only be marked between the daily marking hours. Turn this on to let every Faculty Coordinator and House Captain add or edit attendance any time, all at once. To grant it to just one account instead, use the "After hours" button next to their name below.
